# FortCraft controls

You play Minecraft's world as a TF2 mercenary. Minecraft's keys still move you; TF2 supplies the
weapons, menus and HUD. Every key marked *rebindable* can be changed in Minecraft's
**Options > Controls > Key Binds**, under **FortCraft (TF2)**.

## Moving and fighting

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move (TF2 movement: strafing, air control, rocket and sticky jumps all work) |
| Space | Jump |
| Shift | Crouch |
| Left mouse | Fire your TF2 weapon (melee also breaks the block you aim at) |
| Right mouse | Use the block or mob you aim at (doors, chests, villagers, animals); otherwise your weapon's secondary fire (scope, airblast, detonate stickies, cloak, charge) |
| F | Minecraft's inventory *(rebindable)*. Minecraft's swap-offhand key (normally F) is unbound; set one in Controls if you need it |
| R | TF2 reload *(rebindable)*. Weapons also reload by themselves. With the Eureka Effect out, R opens its teleport menu: 1 = your bed or spawn point, 2 = your teleporter exit, Q = cancel |
| 1-9, mouse wheel | Switch TF2 weapons |
| G | Taunt menu; G again does your weapon's taunt; Q or G stops a taunt *(rebindable)* |
| Z / X / C | TF2 voice menus 1 / 2 / 3; then a number key picks a line, 0 closes *(rebindable)* |

Engineer: with the build or destroy PDA out, 1-4 pick sentry, dispenser, teleporter entrance and
teleporter exit.

## Menus

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| E | Your **backpack**, opened at the page with your Minecraft items *(rebindable)* |
| M | TF2 loadout and items (classes, weapons, cosmetics, crafting) *(rebindable)* |
| , (comma) | Change class *(rebindable)* |
| . (period) | Change team *(rebindable)* |
| P | TF2's main menu *(rebindable)* |
| ` (backtick) | TF2's developer console: type a command, press Enter; ` or Esc closes it *(rebindable)* |
| Hold Tab | TF2 scoreboard |
| Esc | Minecraft's pause menu (or closes the open TF2 menu) |

## Minecraft items in TF2's backpack

Everything in your Minecraft inventory shows on the backpack's last page (E takes you there).

- **Blocks and items:** right-click > **Equip to hand** (or double-click) to hold it. Left-click
  then mines, right-click places or uses it. A weapon key or the wheel puts it away.
- **Health Kit and Ammo Pack:** right-click > **Use** (or double-click). The Health Kit restores
  100-150 health, the Ammo Pack fills your ammo. A kit is given back if you are already full.
- **Food:** there is no hunger. Eat any food (Equip to hand, hold right-click) to heal: raw food
  5-15 health, cooked food 15-50, and it can overheal you.

## Crafting

Open the loadout (M) and choose **Crafting**. Minecraft's 2x2 recipes you can make are listed,
plus FortCraft's supply packs, which are always listed:

| Pack | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Health Kit | 2 string, 2 wool (any colour) |
| Ammo Pack | 9 iron ingots |

Pick a recipe and press **Craft**. If you don't have enough ingredients, the recipe says how many
you have and nothing is taken.

## Good to know

- Survival: Minecraft's mobs hurt your TF2 health; TF2 deaths respawn you at your bed or the world spawn.
- Creative: you can't be hurt, and ammo is unlimited.
- Eating Minecraft food also restores some TF2 health.
